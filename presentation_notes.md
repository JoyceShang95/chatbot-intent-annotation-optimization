# Interview Presentation Notes

## 30-second version
“I made a small overseas customer-service chatbot demo using English consumer queries. I designed the intent taxonomy, annotation rules, boundary cases, error analysis, and knowledge-base fixes. The point is to show the full loop from conversation data → annotation standard → model error → optimization action.”

## What to emphasize from your background
- International customer support in English across email, phone, and chat.
- Experience analyzing customer issues and handling complex cases.
- Participation in AI smart customer-service routing/process optimization.
- Maintaining and updating a multi-product knowledge base.
- Cross-team communication and process improvement.
- Python / PowerShell / Linux as supporting technical capability.

## Avoid saying
- “I trained a real production LLM.”
- “This is a production accuracy benchmark.”

Instead say: “This is a portfolio simulation designed to demonstrate the workflow and my thinking.”
