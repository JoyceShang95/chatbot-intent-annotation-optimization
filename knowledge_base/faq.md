# Knowledge Base — Delivery & Refund FAQ (Demo)

## Where can I track my order?
Use the tracking link in the order details page. If the tracking link is unavailable, provide the latest carrier status.

## My order is late. What should I do?
Check the promised delivery date and latest carrier scan. If the promised date has passed, classify the issue as **delivery_delay** before offering generic tracking instructions.

## Can I cancel an order after it has shipped?
Cancellation eligibility depends on shipment status. Check whether the order has entered fulfillment/shipping before confirming the next step.

## When will I receive my refund?
Check refund status and the refund initiation date. The relevant response should explain whether the refund has been initiated and what processing stage remains.

## I returned an item but have not received my money back.
Once the item has been returned and the customer asks about the money, treat the primary intent as **refund_status**.
